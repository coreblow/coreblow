CoreBlow is a personal AI assistant you run on your own devices.

Pair this iPhone app with your CoreBlow Gateway to connect your phone as a secure node for voice, camera, and device automation.

What you can do:

- Chat with your AI assistant from anywhere
- Use voice commands hands-free
- Share photos, files, and links directly to your agent
- Trigger automations on your gateway
- Receive notifications and updates
- Connect multiple devices to a single gateway

Privacy first:
- Your data stays on your devices
- No cloud processing required
- Full control over what your assistant can access

Requirements:
- A running CoreBlow Gateway (local or remote)
- iOS 17.0 or later
